# Claim verification playbook

## Confidence scale

| Confidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Verified | A primary source confirms the claim, its scope is clear, and no conflicting evidence is known. |
| Likely | The claim is broadly supported, but a relevant definition, method, or scope needs clarification. |
| Uncertain | Evidence is single-source, interested-party, incomplete, or conflicted. |
| Unverified | No primary source can be located; only retellings or assertions are available. |
| Contradicted | Reliable evidence does not support the claim. |

## Source guide

Prefer original documents, official statistics, filings, original datasets, research papers, and original ranking pages. Treat vendor materials, anonymous posts, and social-media claims as leads. A personal judgment is not evidence; label it as an inference.

## Review template

```markdown
## Claim record: [topic]

Review date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

| # | Claim | Priority | Source (type) | Supporting evidence | Gaps / conflicts | Confidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 |  | High |  |  |  |  | Pending |

### Follow-ups
- Claim 1: [missing evidence and next action]

### Overall conclusion
[What can be used as a fact, what needs a qualifier, and what should be omitted.]
```

## Final check

- Can every material claim be traced to a source?
- Are source type and publication date clear?
- Was conflicting evidence actively considered?
- Do numerical claims include scope and measurement context?
- Are inferences visibly separated from facts?
